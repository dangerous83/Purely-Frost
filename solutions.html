Ice Solutions

Purified ice for every industry

Food-grade purified ice engineered to meet the strictest hygiene and cold-chain standards — in every format your business needs.

Reverse OsmosisActivated CarbonUV Sterilized
[ 01 ] THE PLATFORM

A complete cold-chain business solution

Our ice is produced with multi-stage reverse osmosis filtration, activated carbon treatment and UV sterilization — so every bag, block or bulk shipment is safe for direct human consumption.

  • 1KG and 5KG retail bags for sari-sari stores, convenience outlets and food stalls
  • 10KG and 20KG commercial bags for restaurants, catering and event venues
  • Bulk ice by the metric ton with direct fleet delivery for fisheries and industry
  • Same-day or next-day delivery across major population centers
