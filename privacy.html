1. Introduction

Purely Frost Ventures Corporation (“Purely Frost”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit purelyfrost.com (the “Website”), submit a form, respond to our advertisements (including ads on Facebook and Instagram), or otherwise interact with us.

We process personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Where we serve customers in other countries, such as the United Arab Emirates, we also follow applicable local laws.

By using the Website or submitting your information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, apply as a distributor, request our investor deck, subscribe to updates, message us on WhatsApp, or fill in a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address and mobile/phone number
  • Company or organization name and job title
  • City, province or delivery address
  • Your area of interest (e.g., ice orders, distributorship tier, franchise, investment, plant quote)
  • Order volumes, preferred territory, investment range and any message you send us

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Website we and our service providers may collect:

  • IP address, device type, browser type, operating system and language
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, time spent, referring website and approximate location
  • Information from cookies, pixels and similar technologies (see Section 6)

Information from third parties. If you interact with our ads or submit a lead form on Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp), Meta Platforms, Inc. may share with us the information you chose to submit, along with ad campaign data such as which ad you responded to.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (as defined under the Data Privacy Act), and we ask that you do not submit it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotations, product information and customer support
  • To process orders, schedule deliveries and manage supply agreements
  • To evaluate and manage distributor, franchise, partnership and investment applications
  • To send updates, offers and marketing communications where you have agreed to receive them
  • To measure and improve the performance of our Website and advertising, including on Meta platforms
  • To show you relevant ads and build custom or lookalike audiences, where permitted
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud and comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on one or more of the following:

  • Consent — for example, when you submit a form, tick a consent box, or subscribe to updates
  • Contract — to take steps at your request before entering into, or to perform, a sales, supply or distributor agreement
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure and improve our business and Website, in a way that does not override your rights
  • Legal obligation — to comply with tax, accounting, regulatory and other legal requirements

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with:

  • Our group companies — Fourth Pacific Holdings and affiliated entities, including our UAE operations, to serve your inquiry
  • Authorized distributors — the distributor covering your area, so they can fulfil your order or delivery
  • Service providers — website hosting, email, form-handling, CRM, analytics, logistics and payment providers acting on our instructions
  • Advertising partners — such as Meta Platforms, Inc. and Google, to measure ad performance and deliver relevant ads (see Section 6)
  • Authorities — when required by law, court order or to protect the rights, property or safety of Purely Frost, our customers or others
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy

All third parties are required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

6. Cookies, Meta Pixel & Advertising

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, and analytics tools such as Google Analytics. These help us:

  • Understand how visitors use the Website and which pages perform best
  • Measure the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook, Instagram and other platforms
  • Show our ads to people who have visited the Website or are likely to be interested in our products

Meta and other partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to collect or receive information from the Website and elsewhere on the internet and use that information to provide measurement services and target ads. You can learn more and control how Meta uses your information at facebook.com/privacy/policy and manage your ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, opt out of interest-based advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu, and opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the Website work.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy — for example, for the duration of an inquiry or business relationship — and afterwards for as long as required by law (such as tax and accounting rules) or to resolve disputes. Inquiry and lead information that does not result in a business relationship is generally deleted or anonymized within two (2) years. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure and alteration, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and confidentiality obligations for staff and service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to cause you harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

9. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is processed
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Object to processing, including for direct marketing
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure or blocking of information that is unlawfully obtained or no longer necessary
  • Data portability — obtain a copy of your information in a commonly used electronic format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out
  • Damages and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask us to delete information submitted through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, email privacy@purelyfrost.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the period required by law. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email.

10. International Transfers

Because we operate in the Philippines and the United Arab Emirates and use service providers that may be located abroad, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries. When we do so, we take steps to ensure your information remains protected in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable law, including through contractual safeguards.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites and platforms, such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and our parent company’s website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If changes are significant, we will provide a more prominent notice. Your continued use of the Website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
