About FPH Ice Plant

Building the infrastructure of cold

The Philippines’ first national branded purified ice company — built to redefine the standard of ice for human consumption across the archipelago.

Est. under Fourth Pacific HoldingsLuzon · Visayas · MindanaoUAE Operations
[ 01 ] WHO WE ARE

More than an ice company

We produce and distribute hygienic, crystal-clear ice — from 1KG retail bags to large-scale bulk ice — serving communities, businesses and industries nationwide.

Whether you are a fisheries operator in Mindanao, a hotel chain in Metro Manila or a sari-sari store owner in the Visayas, FPH Ice Plant delivers consistent quality and cold-chain excellence to your doorstep. With up to 1,000,000 kilograms of daily capacity and multi-stage filtration with UV sterilization, every bag meets food-grade safety standards before it ever becomes ice.

We are building a nationwide network of distributors, franchise partners and production facilities that keeps food fresh, businesses running and communities cool.
