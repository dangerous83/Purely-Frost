Orders & Delivery

Submit a request through our Get a Quote form, call us, or chat with us on WhatsApp. Our sales team will confirm pricing, volume and a delivery schedule for your location.

We serve Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao through our distributor network, with same-day or next-day delivery across major population centers. Contact us to confirm coverage in your area.

Minimums depend on your location and delivery route. Retail partners typically order by the bag, while commercial and industrial clients order by volume or metric ton.

Products & Purity

Our purified tube ice comes in 1KG, 2KG, 5KG, 10KG and 20KG bags, plus bulk ice by the metric ton.

Yes. Our ice is made for human consumption using multi-stage reverse osmosis, activated carbon and UV sterilization, with microbial testing on every batch.

Hollow tube ice is crystal-clear, melts slowly and evenly, and cools drinks efficiently — ideal for beverages and food service.

Keep bags sealed and stored at -18°C or below. Avoid repeated thawing and refreezing to maintain clarity and quality.

Distributors & Partnerships

Choose a tier — Silver, Gold or Platinum — and submit an application. We confirm territory availability, evaluate fit, and onboard approved partners with training and branded collateral.

Silver covers local/barangay territories at around 500 kg/day; Gold covers municipal/city territories at around 2,000 kg/day; Platinum covers provincial/regional territories at 5,000+ kg/day with sub-licensing rights.

Yes. Every tier includes a path to grow upward as your volume and capacity increase.

Yes. All tiers include exclusive territory rights, subject to availability and approval.
