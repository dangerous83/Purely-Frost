[ 03 ] FILTRATION

Multi-stage treatment

Our water treatment train removes sediment, chlorine, dissolved solids and microorganisms before a single drop is frozen.

  • Sediment pre-filtration — removes sand, rust and particulates
  • Activated carbon — eliminates chlorine, odor and taste
  • Reverse osmosis — strips dissolved solids for crystal-clear output
  • UV sterilization — neutralizes bacteria and viruses
